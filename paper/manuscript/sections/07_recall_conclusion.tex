\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Archived KG selection exhibits family-specific recall and size profiles under different evidence conditions. Explicit relations greatly increase coverage while leaving model-specific omissions; matched-fact presentation changes introduce both corrections and regressions. These analyses deepen an existing Tool-to-KG comparison by separating relation availability from residual selection failures. They motivate tests of dependency inference, route coverage, and distractor rejection without establishing shared internal causes or downstream QA gains.
