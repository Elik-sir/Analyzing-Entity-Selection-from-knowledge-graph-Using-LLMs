# Related Work and Prior Transfer Evidence

**Structural difficulty and prior Tool-to-KG evidence.** Tool Graph Complexity distinguishes reference-set non-minimality from dependency composition and analyzes motif-associated tool-selection difficulty ([Tool Graph Complexity](/Users/ivan_kirpichev/Projects/Analyzing-Entity-Selection-from-knowledge-graph-Using-LLMs/paper/research_notes/sources/tool_graph_complexity.pdf)). Its adjusted analysis emphasizes sequential-pattern penalties, whereas raw motif rankings also reflect size and surface properties ([Tool Graph Complexity](/Users/ivan_kirpichev/Projects/Analyzing-Entity-Selection-from-knowledge-graph-Using-LLMs/paper/research_notes/sources/tool_graph_complexity.pdf)). Appendix O already evaluates entity selection on a finance KG, including motif and size variation, explicit relations, and adjacent decoys; it reports easier chains with explicit KG relations, difficult incoming trees, and decoy-related inclusion errors ([Tool Graph Complexity](/Users/ivan_kirpichev/Projects/Analyzing-Entity-Selection-from-knowledge-graph-Using-LLMs/paper/research_notes/sources/tool_graph_complexity.pdf)). These are antecedent findings for our diagnostic follow-up, which separates relation disclosure from matched-fact presentation and examines residual selection errors. Differences in references, prompts, populations, and evaluation protocols limit direct score comparisons; unquantified task/response overlap also precludes a claim of independent replication.

**Graph structure and task comprehension.** Talk like a Graph evaluates how graph encodings, task types, and graph structure affect LLM performance ([Fatemi et al., 2024](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/bf72f65f30eedf5d48da6980ee02b589-Abstract-Conference.html)). Xinnan Dai et al. study pattern comprehension and discovery from terminological and topological descriptions, including pattern detection and graph modification ([Xinnan Dai et al., 2025](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/9316da9c25ab559ba678f2fe52217a64-Abstract-Conference.html)). GraphOmni jointly varies graph families, serialization formats, and prompting schemes across graph-theoretic tasks ([Xu et al., 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.12764v5)). Our motifs organize comparisons of query-conditioned entity selection: the reference includes intermediate and branching entities on all permitted supporting routes. The analysis concerns recovery of this declared route reference, with structure interpreted alongside query semantics and visible evidence.

**Availability and representation of relational evidence.** Xinbang Dai et al. compare KG input formats and triple organization and evaluate robustness to noisy or incomplete subgraphs using answer-based evaluation ([Xinbang Dai et al., 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.11541v4)). Tian et al. distinguish graph transformation from linearization and examine formats, orders, and templates in KG-augmented generation ([Tian et al., 2025](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/34716)). We distinguish adding explicit relations to native entity cards from changing the presentation of designated matched facts. Raw versus Raw+E changes available information; controlled triples versus templated prose and source-, destination-, or both-card hosting change how that information is presented. Native descriptions are not assumed to express every graph relation, so disclosure gains alone cannot establish better reasoning from equally informative inputs.

**Graph retrieval and route recovery.** STaRK evaluates retrieval of answer entities satisfying textual and relational query constraints ([Wu et al., 2024](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/e607b1419e9ae7cd5cb5b5bb60c2ad5c-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.html)). G-Retriever constructs graph context using prize-collecting Steiner tree optimization, while Reasoning on Graphs plans relation sequences and retrieves instantiated reasoning paths for KG question answering ([He et al., 2024](https://proceedings.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/efaf1c9726648c8ba363a5c927440529-Abstract-Conference.html); [Luo et al., 2024](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/3e2aeb66481dd63a32421bf032b70384-Abstract-Conference.html)). GraphWalk studies tool-based graph navigation and reports incomplete exploration in its graph-in-context setting ([Ghandi et al., 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.01610v1)). Our main evaluation holds target availability within a supplied candidate pool fixed and scores selected entity sets against the complete route reference. This objective differs from retrieving answer entities, constructing a compact useful subgraph, or generating an answer from retrieved paths ([Wu et al., 2024](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/e607b1419e9ae7cd5cb5b5bb60c2ad5c-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.html); [He et al., 2024](https://proceedings.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/efaf1c9726648c8ba363a5c927440529-Abstract-Conference.html); [Luo et al., 2024](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/3e2aeb66481dd63a32421bf032b70384-Abstract-Conference.html)). Final-set omissions are observable selection errors; they do not reconstruct internal traversal steps or identify a shared failure mechanism across these settings.

**Distractors and selection metrics.** Amiraz et al. quantify how irrelevant passages can distract answer-generating models, distinguishing distraction from a binary relevance label ([Amiraz et al., 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.892/)). TopoRAG addresses noise from heuristic neighbor expansion through retrieval constrained by induced-subgraph diameter ([Wu and Luo, 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1703/)). Our disconnected/adjacent-context comparison instead examines query-irrelevant neighboring entities within a fixed-size pool containing every reference entity. Structural proximity is a different distractor construction from passage-level semantic distraction and does not by itself establish irrelevance ([Amiraz et al., 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.892/)). Li and Ouyang show that the relationship between knowledge-selection quality and generation quality depends on the generator and task ([Li and Ouyang, 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.218/)). Accordingly, we use recall to measure route coverage and precision and exact recovery to diagnose excess selection and complete-set recovery; these metrics establish neither minimum sufficient evidence nor downstream answer correctness.
