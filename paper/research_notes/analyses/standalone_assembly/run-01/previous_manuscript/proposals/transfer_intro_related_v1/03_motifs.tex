\subsection{Directed Task Families}
\label{sec:motifs}
We study seven directed families: chain, star-in, star-out, tree-in, tree-out, rhombus, and hourglass. Figure~\ref{fig:raw_motifs} gives a compact key alongside Raw/Raw+E recall. Arrows denote factual KG relations; incoming queries inspect them in the reverse direction. Each query names an anchor, which belongs to the all-route selection reference together with the endpoints and intermediate entities. The Core sizes are six nodes for chain, stars, and trees, four for rhombus, and five for hourglass. Full graph definitions, traversal rules, and the distinction between endpoint answers and selected route entities appear in Appendix~\ref{app:motifs}. The families also differ in query operations and entity/relation types.
