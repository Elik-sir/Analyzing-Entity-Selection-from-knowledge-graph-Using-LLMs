\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Knowledge-graph entity selection exhibits task-family profiles that node count alone does not describe. On the archived common-size tasks, incoming trees are markedly less often recovered exactly than outward stars. Across available sizes, several chains and trees lose quality while other profiles remain near ceiling or vary nonmonotonically. Explicit relations greatly increase graph-reference recovery, yet adjacent contexts still expose omission and excess-selection errors. These findings support evaluating structural profiles under clearly specified evidence conditions, with partial and exact recovery reported together. They do not establish a universal topology ranking, indispensable-card minimality, or downstream QA gains.
