7 Conclusion
Archived KG selection exhibits family-specific recall and size
profiles under different evidence conditions. Explicit relations
greatly increase coverage while leaving model-specific omissions;
matched-fact presentation changes introduce both corrections and
regressions. These analyses deepen an existing Tool-to-KG com-
parison by separating relation availability from residual selection
failures. They motivate tests of dependency inference, route cover-
age, and distractor rejection without establishing shared internal
causes or downstream QA gains.
