A Additional Metrics and Condition
Definitions
Core uses pseudonyms, procedural questions, and disconnected
distractors unless specified. K1 is source-card triples; K2 adds adja-
cent distractors; K3 hosts triples on destination cards; K4 repeats
them on both endpoint cards. K5 is templated text; K6 is its ad-
jacent variant. K7/K8 use direct queries with triples/text. K5k ex-
presses links through matching attribute values. R1-matched/R2-
matched use real names with disconnected/adjacent triples. R0/K0
retain names/types only. Their prompt permits prior name knowl-
edge and requests an insufficient-evidence flag; other controlled
conditions restrict evidence to supplied cards.
Fact-preserving pairs retain designated unique relations and tar-
gets. Noise pairs retain the question/reference while altering dis-
tractor context. A change in presentation may still alter repetition,
salience, and token length. Native metadata is not assumed suffi-
cient for every graph-defined target.
Output signatures and diagnostic limits. An exact one-hop sig-
nature means the saved selection equals the anchor plus all per-
mitted first-hop neighbours. On the inspected Qwen K1 six-node
incoming-tree tasks, this occurs in 71/100 outputs; K3/K4/K7 have
76/31/52. Their exact-reference counts are 16/51/16, compared with
15 in K1. This distinguishes elimination of a signature from cor-
rection of the task. All these Qwen cells have 100 valid outcomes.
The same original 71 one-hop outputs become exact on 13 K3, 38
K4, and ten K7 tasks. Under K4, six initially exact outputs lose ex-
actness. No new interval or significance test is attached to these
post-hoc contrasts.
Ministral has 15/100 initial one-hop sets and 54/100 exact out-
puts in K1. K4 makes eight of those one-hop sets exact and loses ex-
actness on nine initially exact tasks; total exact recovery is 62/100.
Gemma’s aggregate scores remain in all main tables/figures, but
the archive lacks identities for this finer check. Output signatures
do not prove hidden search depth or branch order.
On the inspected Qwen K1 incoming-tree inputs, undirected
depth-two and directed depth-three coverage agree with the cor-
rect reference on all 100 tasks. Under K2 these alternative targets
differ on 97/100 and 10/100 tasks, respectively; no Qwen output
exactly matches those distinct alternative sets. This does not rule
out mixed or partial rule violations. This slice has no terminal-type
stopping condition, so it cannot diagnose terminal-type errors.
Analytical and uncertainty context. Select-all has recall one on
every nonempty reference by definition, and precision equal to the
reference proportion in the 25-candidate pool. These are analyt-
ical reference rules, not executed LLM/retrieval baselines. A full
connected-component baseline was not computed. Earlier archive
uncertainty analyses cover F1/exact with anchor-conditional as-
sumptions; no such bands are reused for the present recall/preci-
sion results. Original exact scores, earlier metrics, native/legacy
panels, and incomplete incremental histories remain in the sup-
porting source artifacts rather than being pooled into the main
populations.
B Author-Supplied Cypher Extraction
Specification
The following Cypher query was supplied by the authors for the
appendix. Whitespace is normalized and comments are translated
into English for display; executable tokens match the saved origi-
nal. It enumerates small instances of nine directed pattern defini-
tions, requires distinct vertices, and excludes additional directed
typed edges inside each selected vertex set, including self-loops.
The comparison uses source/type/target triples, so parallel physi-
cal relationships with the same triple are not distinguished by the
extra-edge test.

name acceptance filters, or the complete archived size grid. The
