## 6. Discussion

Organize by inference strength: what was observed; which operation-level hypotheses it supports; which alternatives remain; what would falsify the explanation. Discuss the transfer inference explicitly: KG can help formulate or test boundary hypotheses, but cross-domain score differences and similar final errors do not establish a shared internal mechanism. Explain evidence incompleteness, route-union versus conjunctive computation, redundancy/salience, query/type confounds, dependent tasks, historical metadata, reuse and downstream limits. A new real-tool dependency-overlay experiment would directly test the prior explanation; a proposed experiment is not a result.

## 7. Conclusion
