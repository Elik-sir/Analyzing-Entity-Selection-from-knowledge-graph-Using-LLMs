# Initial methodology and contribution candidates

Status: provisional synthesis of existing materials, not an approved final scientific claim set.

## Definitions to settle

Distinguish candidate pool C, sampled motif M, endpoint answer A, and all-route witness set W. Current controlled prompts/oracles support all-route coverage; archive-specific equivalence between M and W needs checking. Minimal graph support and indispensable visible cards are additional notions. Native metadata is not automatically sufficient for a graph reference; final answer correctness is separate from selection quality.

## RQs and evidence routes

- Evidence availability: paired `full_braw` Braw/Braw+E rows, same-base input/reference audit, failure-inclusive set metrics. This adds information; native answerability constrains interpretation.
- Representation of controlled facts: Core K1/K5 and K7/K8, plus appropriate key-format conditions. Verify matched facts, question semantics and populations. Templated prose is not unconstrained natural text.
- Structural/task conditions: disconnected/adjacent pairs and motif/size strata. Motif comparisons change query operations; sizes are not uniformly covered across models. Avoid a pure-topology causal claim.
- Naming, placement, and access: use only the relevant archived contrasts. Aliases change tokenization; incomplete incremental histories restrict access-protocol conclusions.

## Candidate contributions

1. A clearly scoped fixed-pool entity route-coverage evaluation, distinguished from final QA and minimal evidence selection.
2. Matched comparisons of visible evidence, controlled rendering and distractor conditions, to the extent archived pairs substantiate the design.
3. An empirical account of selection failures with reusable archived observations and explicit provenance/evidence limitations.

These candidates require literature comparison. Prior corporate KG construction belongs to the earlier work. No component is declared novel here.

## Decision

Keep the full-writing gate closed while the first-pass discrepancies are triaged, reference/input matching is audited, planned claims receive coverage, and literature positioning is verified. A supported outline may be prepared now. Missing historical material can be handled by narrower claims and transparent limitations; not every gap requires a new experiment.
