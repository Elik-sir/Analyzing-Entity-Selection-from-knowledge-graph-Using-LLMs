# Initial experiment audit

2026-10-25. Read-only archive inspection and bounded standard-library aggregation; no model calls, judge calls, source edits or full experiment reruns. Numeric outputs and their source hashes are in `analyses/archive_audit/summary.json`, generated by `scripts/audit_saved_archive.py`.

## Evidence and scoring

The fresh audit verifies file sizes/SHA-256 against `results/MANIFEST.json` and streams `results/observations.jsonl.gz`. It checks archive structure and aggregates cached metrics; it does not independently rescore all responses. `results/VALIDATION.json` is a saved earlier verification and must be labelled as such.

Join observations to payloads by `input_id` and `response_id`; native task specs use `record_id` against `base_id`. Response payload count is not the number of independent observations because stages may have separate responses. Resolve source IDs through `SOURCES.json`; original source paths need not exist locally.

Primary means follow `graph_motives/report.py:aggregates`: sum valid task metrics / planned N. Invalid/missing outcomes stay in N and contribute zero; missing individual metrics are not silently converted in the source archive. Final answer correctness and entity-selection metrics are separate.

The summary retains exact series/model/condition/noise selectors, N, valid counts and means for Full Braw. These are descriptive archived differences only. No significance test, paired confidence interval, causal identification, or downstream QA evaluation was performed here. Rounding for prose should be applied only after aggregation.

## Coverage and limits

- `coverage.csv` and `VALIDATION.json` distinguish raw responses, frozen inputs, metrics-only outcomes and reconstructed requests. Gemma controlled outcomes include cached metrics without raw replies; Ministral Braw includes exhausted attempts without a received response. See exact counts and coverage in the source records, not inferred successful-call totals.
- Incremental inputs preserve initial context, not complete historical turn sequences. They cannot support a fresh turn-by-turn replay claim.
- Declared configuration and authenticated provider/request metadata have different evidential strength. Current model templates cannot fill historical omissions.
- `SOURCES.json` records provenance to another source tree; checksum-verification flags do not imply those original files are now present.
